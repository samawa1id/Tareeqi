# Tareeqi (طريقي)

**Report it. Repair it. Protect everyone.**
*Smarter Roads. Safer Lives.*

Tareeqi is a mobile-first web app that lets drivers in Egypt report road hazards in under a minute, confirm hazards reported by others, and follow each report until the road is repaired. Built for the ALX final project.

**Live demo:** _https://tareeqi-mvp.vercel.app_

## The problem

Damaged roads, broken signs, dead streetlights and flooding cause accidents every day. Drivers see these hazards but have no quick way to report them. Roads departments have no clear, ranked list of what needs fixing first, and nobody can see whether a report led to a repair.

## The solution

Tareeqi connects the people who see hazards with the people who fix them:

1. **Report it:** pick the hazard, set the risk level, add a photo and drop a pin.
2. **Repair it:** reports reach the team responsible for that road, with the exact location and the number of drivers who confirmed it.
3. **Protect everyone:** other drivers see the alert and avoid it until it is fixed.

## Features

- **Report a problem:** six categories, three risk levels, optional photo and description.
- **Location picker:** tap the map or use GPS. The street and district are worked out from the pin.
- **Alerts map:** a map of Greater Cairo with zoom, pan, filters and tappable markers.
- **Report tracking:** a four-stage tracker (Reported, Verified, Assigned, Repaired).
- **Community confirmation:** drivers confirm hazards with "I see this too".
- **My reports:** every report you submitted and its current status.
- **Authority mode:** a demo switch that moves reports through the repair stages, simulating a roads department.
